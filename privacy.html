This Privacy Policy explains what data the Discord bot Lykosaris ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it's handled when you use the Bot in a Discord server ("Server") or interact with it directly. It applies to the Bot only; how Discord itself handles your data is described in Discord's Privacy Policy.

By using Lykosaris, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the Bot and ask a server administrator to remove it.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data that is necessary for the Bot's features to work, and only for the features that you or your server's staff actually use. This is limited to:

Identifiers

  • Your Discord User ID and, where relevant, the Server (Guild), Channel, Role, Message, and Thread IDs where a feature was configured or used.
  • A few features also keep a name as it appeared at the time (for example, /contactstaff conversations keep the username of each participant and the name of the Server).
  • We do not collect your email address, IP address, phone number, or real name — Discord IDs are the only identifiers we use.

Content you provide to specific features

Some features require you (or server staff) to submit text or images, which are stored only for that feature to work:

  • /profile Your "About Me" text (up to 200 characters), colors, equipped backgrounds, semi-transparent box settings, and — if you have Lykosaris Premium — your frame, ribbon text, and card format choices.
  • Tickets The reason and the answers you give to a ticket's form questions, its priority, and who claimed it. When a ticket is closed, a transcript of its messages is generated and sent as a text file to the Server's log channel (if one is configured). That transcript is kept by the Server in Discord; it is not stored in the Bot's database.
  • /contactstaff The messages exchanged between you and server staff through that feature (text, author name and ID, and date). These messages are also sent as direct messages to you and to the staff members with the configured role(s).
  • Trading & Store The title, description, terms, and optional image link of trade listings you create, who claimed them, their status, and the likes/dislikes left as trade reputation; and the product names, descriptions, price text, and stock of store listings.
  • Teams A team's name, tag, description, emoji, leader, admins, members, and join requests (who asked to join and whether it was accepted, declined, or cancelled).
  • Giveaways The user IDs of the people who entered a giveaway and of its winners.
  • /afk Your AFK status in each Server and the optional message you set for it.
  • Birthdays The day and month of your birthday (no year), if you choose to set it. It is stored once and applies to every Server where you and the Bot are present. Servers that have a birthday channel configured may announce your birthday there, visible to that channel's members.
  • Language Your personal language preference, if you set one.
  • Quiz Game Quiz questions and answers created by staff. Quizzes use Discord's native polls; we only read the total number of votes for the correct answer once a poll ends, never who voted for what.
  • /roleplay The family bonds you create with other users: for each bond, the Discord IDs of the two people, its type (partner, marriage, parent and child, or sibling), who the parent is when applicable, and the date it started (plus the wedding date, for marriages). Bonds are not tied to a Server: they follow you in every Server where the Bot is present. Pending requests are stored too (who sent it, who received it, the type, the ID of the Server where it was sent, and the date), and so is your choice to stop receiving new requests. Other people see bonds as a family tree (see section 2).

Activity & progression data

  • Server-specific level, XP, and prestige progress, the time of your last XP-earning message (used for the XP cooldown), and an XP history of the last 90 days (used for the weekly and monthly rankings; older entries are deleted automatically).
  • "LYKO Coins" balance (shared across Servers), purchases, inventory (owned and equipped backgrounds), and the redeem codes you have used.
  • Top.gg voting data: your total number of votes and the time of your last vote, received from Top.gg's vote notifications, used to show your vote cooldown and reward your vote.
  • Counting-game statistics, per Server: the current count, record, and number of failures, plus who sent the last number, who broke the count, who used a "save", and which users are temporarily banned from the game.
  • Temporary voice channels: who owns each channel, its privacy and chat settings, and its lists of trusted and blocked users.

Lykosaris Premium

  • Whether you have an active Lykosaris Premium subscription, its tier, and when it started and expires or renews. This status is provided to us automatically by Discord when a subscription is created, renewed, or cancelled; it can also be granted manually by us (for example, as a gift). When you subscribe, we may send you a direct message to thank you.
  • We never see or store your payment details (card number, bank details, billing address, etc.). All payment processing is handled directly between you, Discord, and Discord's payment provider — we only receive the subscription status itself.

Moderation records

  • Warnings issued to you by server staff (reason, date, issuing moderator), visible to that server's moderators.
  • Kicks, bans, and timeouts issued with the Bot's moderation commands (type, reason, date, and issuing moderator), and, for temporary bans, the time at which the ban ends.
  • Aggregate moderation action counts (e.g. how many kicks/bans a moderator has issued), used for staff statistics only.
  • Actions taken by the Bot's automatic safety tools (see section 2) are reported in the Server's configured log channels.

Server configuration

  • Settings configured by server administrators (channel IDs, role IDs, custom messages and embeds, welcome and goodbye messages, autoresponders, word lists, feature toggles, and the accounts a Server follows for social media notifications). This is configuration data about the Server, not about individual members.
  • When the Bot is removed from a Server, we record that Server's ID, name, member count, and the date of removal.

Operational logs

  • The Bot's hosting provider keeps technical logs of the Bot's operation (for example, error messages and the IDs involved in an event). Requests that reach the Bot over the internet, such as notifications from Top.gg or YouTube, may also be logged by the hosting provider with the sender's network information.

2. How the Bot Processes Messages and Members

To work, the Bot receives message and member events from Discord. Message content is processed in real time to check whether it matters to an active feature and, unless a feature below says otherwise, it is not saved. Only the result is stored (for example, XP gained or the next number in a counting game).

  • Message-based features such as leveling, the counting game, landmines, autoresponders, AFK notices, sticky messages, and the honeypot check messages as they arrive to decide whether to act.
  • Edited and deleted message log (optional). If a server administrator enables it, the Bot posts the text of messages that members edit or delete (their author, content before and after, channel, and any attachments, which the Bot downloads and uploads again) to the log channel that the administrator chose, so staff can see what was said. This log lives in that Server's Discord channel and is visible to whoever has access to it; it is not saved in the Bot's database. Logging may be incomplete for messages the Bot could not see.
  • Anti-raid. If enabled by an administrator, the Bot briefly keeps in memory (about one minute, never in the database) how many invite links and images each user has posted, and may delete those messages and time out, kick, or ban the user according to the Server's settings.
  • Bot scanner. If enabled, when someone joins, the Bot may check publicly visible account information (how recently the account was created, whether it has a profile picture, public badges, and the Server tag) and flag suspicious accounts to staff with the option to kick or ban them. This information is not stored.
  • Server tag role. If configured, the Bot checks whether a member shows the Server's tag on their profile in order to give or remove a role. This is not stored.
  • Forbidden words. The word lists configured by administrators are enforced by Discord's own AutoMod through rules the Bot creates; the Bot does not scan messages for them itself.
  • Media tools. Images you submit to commands like /media imagetogif are downloaded temporarily to be converted and are not stored.
  • Family trees (Roleplay). When someone uses the family tree command, or its button in the Roleplay menu, the Bot draws an image with the usernames and profile pictures of the people in that family tree and posts it publicly in the channel. By default it only shows the people who are in that Server (everyone else appears as an anonymous silhouette with a "?"); the owner of a tree can choose to show people from all Servers. To draw it, the Bot checks which people are members of the Server and downloads profile pictures from Discord, keeping them in memory for up to about 10 minutes. The image itself is not stored by the Bot.
  • Roleplay notifications. When you send someone a Roleplay request, the Bot posts a message in the channel where you sent it, mentioning the recipient (so the members of that channel can see it), and sends them a direct message. When a request is accepted, the person who sent it receives a direct message. Nobody is notified when a request is declined or when a bond is removed.

We do not read, log, or store the content of your regular messages beyond what is described above. We do not track your activity outside of the specific commands and features you use, and we do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the features you or your server's staff choose to use (leveling, economy, tickets, moderation tools, games, notifications, etc).
  • To keep server-specific statistics (e.g. member/bot counters, rankings) up to date.
  • To provide Lykosaris Premium and handle related support requests.
  • To provide Roleplay: family bonds, requests, and family trees.
  • To prevent abuse of the Bot (e.g. anti-raid detection, cooldowns, limits on rewards and redeem codes).

4. Where Your Data Is Stored and Third Parties

Data is stored in a MongoDB database and processed by the Bot's application code, hosted on a third-party cloud provider (Render). These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

The Bot also connects to the following services. Except where noted, only public identifiers (such as a channel name or an app ID chosen by a server administrator) are sent, not personal data about members:

  • Discord — the platform the Bot runs on.
  • Top.gg — the Bot reports its total server count and receives vote notifications (including the voter's user ID).
  • YouTube / Google, Twitch, Steam, and TikTok — used for social media notifications, by looking up the public channels, usernames, or games that a Server's administrators asked to follow.
  • GitHub — the Bot downloads public emoji image files from it to draw images (such as profile and ranking cards). No personal data is sent.
  • A game server statistics service — used only to show a player counter in a Server's statistics channel. No personal data is sent.
  • Ko-fi — the /donate command only shows a link. Donations are handled entirely by Ko-fi, and the Bot does not receive any payment details.

No data is knowingly transferred to any other third party except as described in this policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Data tied to a Server (settings, levels, tickets, etc.) is generally retained for as long as the Bot remains in that Server.
  • Server-specific data is not currently deleted automatically when the Bot is removed from a Server; it can be deleted on request (see section 6).
  • Global data tied to your Discord account (e.g. LYKO Coins, your birthday, your language preference, and vote counts) persists across servers for as long as your account has interacted with the Bot, unless deletion is requested.
  • XP history is limited to the last 90 days.
  • Roleplay bonds stay until one of the two people removes them (or until deletion is requested); a removed bond is deleted and no history of it is kept. Pending Roleplay requests do not expire: they stay until they are accepted, declined, or cancelled. If a Discord account is deleted, its bonds can remain until someone removes them, and that account appears as "deleted" in family trees.
  • Content that server staff or the Bot post in a Server's channels (such as ticket transcripts and message logs) is kept in Discord according to that Server's own settings and is not controlled by us.

6. Your Rights

You can request, at any time:

  • Access — a summary of the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion — removal of your personal data from our database.
  • Correction — fixing inaccurate data (where applicable).
  • Objection or restriction — asking us to stop or limit how we use your data, where the law gives you that right.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 10. We may need to verify your Discord identity before processing a request, and we will reply within a reasonable time.

You can remove your own Roleplay bonds and pending requests yourself at any time from the Roleplay menu. We can also delete all of your Roleplay data on request.

Please note that some data is controlled by the Server's staff rather than by us (for example, ticket transcripts, message logs, and warnings visible to moderators). We can delete what is in the Bot's database, but you may also need to ask that Server's staff to remove content they hold in Discord. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the applicable age of digital consent in their country). Lykosaris does not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age. If you believe a minor has provided us data in violation of this policy, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data we hold and limit access to what is necessary to run the Bot. However, no online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the revised policy.

2. Cómo procesa el Bot los mensajes y los miembros

Para funcionar, el Bot recibe de Discord eventos de mensajes y de miembros. El contenido de los mensajes se procesa en tiempo real para comprobar si importa a alguna función activa y, salvo que una de las funciones siguientes diga lo contrario, no se guarda. Solo se almacena el resultado (por ejemplo, la XP ganada o el siguiente número de un juego de contar).

  • Funciones basadas en mensajes, como los niveles, el juego de contar, las minas, las autorrespuestas, los avisos de AFK, los mensajes fijos (sticky) y el honeypot, revisan los mensajes según llegan para decidir si actuar.
  • Registro de mensajes editados y borrados (opcional). Si un administrador del servidor lo activa, el Bot publica el texto de los mensajes que los miembros editan o borran (su autor, el contenido antes y después, el canal y los adjuntos, que el Bot descarga y vuelve a subir) en el canal de registros que eligió el administrador, para que el personal pueda ver qué se dijo. Este registro vive en el canal de Discord de ese Servidor y es visible para quien tenga acceso a él; no se guarda en la base de datos del Bot. El registro puede ser incompleto en los mensajes que el Bot no pudo ver.
  • Antiraid. Si lo activa un administrador, el Bot guarda brevemente en memoria (alrededor de un minuto, nunca en la base de datos) cuántos enlaces de invitación e imágenes ha publicado cada usuario, y puede borrar esos mensajes y aislar (timeout), expulsar o banear al usuario según los ajustes del Servidor.
  • Bot scanner. Si está activado, cuando alguien se une, el Bot puede comprobar información pública de la cuenta (hace cuánto se creó, si tiene foto de perfil, insignias públicas y el tag del Servidor) y señalar cuentas sospechosas al personal, con la opción de expulsarlas o banearlas. Esta información no se guarda.
  • Rol de server tag. Si está configurado, el Bot comprueba si un miembro muestra el tag del Servidor en su perfil para darle o quitarle un rol. Esto no se guarda.
  • Palabras prohibidas. Las listas de palabras configuradas por los administradores las aplica el AutoMod de Discord mediante reglas que crea el Bot; el Bot no analiza los mensajes por su cuenta para esto.
  • Herramientas multimedia. Las imágenes que envías a comandos como /media imagetogif se descargan temporalmente para convertirlas y no se guardan.
  • Árboles familiares (Roleplay). Cuando alguien usa el comando del árbol familiar, o su botón en el menú de Roleplay, el Bot dibuja una imagen con los nombres de usuario y las fotos de perfil de las personas de ese árbol y la publica a la vista de todos en el canal. Por defecto solo muestra a las personas que están en ese Servidor (el resto aparece como una silueta anónima con un "?"); el propietario de un árbol puede elegir mostrar a las personas de todos los Servidores. Para dibujarlo, el Bot comprueba qué personas son miembros del Servidor y descarga las fotos de perfil desde Discord, y las conserva en memoria hasta unos 10 minutos. El Bot no guarda la imagen.
  • Avisos de Roleplay. Cuando envías una solicitud de Roleplay a alguien, el Bot publica un mensaje en el canal desde el que la enviaste, mencionando a esa persona (así que los miembros de ese canal pueden verlo), y le manda un mensaje directo. Cuando se acepta una solicitud, quien la envió recibe un mensaje directo. No se avisa a nadie cuando se rechaza una solicitud ni cuando se elimina un vínculo.

No leemos, registramos ni guardamos el contenido de tus mensajes normales más allá de lo descrito arriba. No rastreamos tu actividad fuera de los comandos y funciones concretos que uses, y no vendemos, alquilamos ni compartimos tus datos con anunciantes ni intermediarios de datos.

3. Cómo usamos tus datos

  • Para hacer funcionar las funciones que tú o el personal de tu servidor elijan usar (niveles, economía, tickets, herramientas de moderación, juegos, notificaciones, etc.).
  • Para mantener al día las estadísticas de cada servidor (p. ej., contadores de miembros y bots, rankings).
  • Para ofrecer Lykosaris Premium y atender las solicitudes de soporte relacionadas.
  • Para ofrecer Roleplay: vínculos familiares, solicitudes y árboles familiares.
  • Para prevenir abusos del Bot (p. ej., detección de raids, tiempos de espera, límites en recompensas y códigos de canje).

4. Dónde se guardan tus datos y terceros

Los datos se guardan en una base de datos MongoDB y los procesa el código de la aplicación del Bot, alojado en un proveedor de nube de terceros (Render). Estos proveedores tratan los datos en nuestro nombre conforme a sus propios términos y políticas de privacidad.

El Bot también se conecta a los siguientes servicios. Salvo que se indique lo contrario, solo se envían identificadores públicos (como el nombre de un canal o un ID de aplicación elegido por un administrador del servidor), no datos personales de los miembros:

  • Discord: la plataforma en la que funciona el Bot.
  • Top.gg: el Bot informa de su número total de servidores y recibe notificaciones de voto (incluido el ID de usuario de quien vota).
  • YouTube / Google, Twitch, Steam y TikTok: se usan para las notificaciones de redes sociales, consultando los canales, usuarios o juegos públicos que los administradores de un Servidor pidieron seguir.
  • GitHub: el Bot descarga de allí archivos públicos de imágenes de emojis para dibujar imágenes (como las tarjetas de perfil y de ranking). No se envían datos personales.
  • Un servicio de estadísticas de un servidor de juego: se usa solo para mostrar un contador de jugadores en el canal de estadísticas de un Servidor. No se envían datos personales.
  • Ko-fi: el comando /donate solo muestra un enlace. Las donaciones las gestiona íntegramente Ko-fi, y el Bot no recibe ningún dato de pago.

No se transfieren conscientemente datos a ningún otro tercero salvo lo descrito en esta política.

5. Conservación de los datos

  • Los datos vinculados a un Servidor (ajustes, niveles, tickets, etc.) se conservan, por lo general, mientras el Bot siga en ese Servidor.
  • Los datos específicos de un Servidor no se borran automáticamente por ahora cuando se quita el Bot de él; se pueden borrar a petición (ver sección 6).
  • Los datos globales vinculados a tu cuenta de Discord (p. ej., LYKO Coins, tu cumpleaños, tu preferencia de idioma y el número de votos) persisten entre servidores mientras tu cuenta haya interactuado con el Bot, salvo que se solicite su borrado.
  • El historial de XP se limita a los últimos 90 días.
  • Los vínculos de Roleplay se conservan hasta que una de las dos personas los elimina (o hasta que se solicite su borrado); un vínculo eliminado se borra y no se guarda ningún historial de él. Las solicitudes pendientes de Roleplay no caducan: se quedan hasta que se aceptan, se rechazan o se cancelan. Si se elimina una cuenta de Discord, sus vínculos pueden permanecer hasta que alguien los quite, y esa cuenta aparece como "eliminado" en los árboles familiares.
  • El contenido que el personal del servidor o el Bot publican en los canales de un Servidor (como las transcripciones de tickets y los registros de mensajes) se conserva en Discord según los ajustes de ese Servidor y no lo controlamos nosotros.

6. Tus derechos

Puedes solicitar en cualquier momento:

  • Acceso: un resumen de los datos que tenemos sobre ti.
  • Eliminación: que borremos tus datos personales de nuestra base de datos.
  • Rectificación: que corrijamos datos inexactos (cuando proceda).
  • Oposición o limitación: que dejemos de usar tus datos o limitemos su uso, cuando la ley te dé ese derecho.

Para ejercer cualquiera de estos derechos, contáctanos con los datos de la sección 10. Puede que necesitemos verificar tu identidad en Discord antes de tramitar una solicitud, y responderemos en un plazo razonable.

Puedes eliminar tú mismo/a tus vínculos y solicitudes pendientes de Roleplay en cualquier momento desde el menú de Roleplay. También podemos borrar todos tus datos de Roleplay si nos lo pides.

Ten en cuenta que algunos datos los controla el personal del Servidor y no nosotros (por ejemplo, las transcripciones de tickets, los registros de mensajes y los avisos visibles para los moderadores). Nosotros podemos borrar lo que esté en la base de datos del Bot, pero quizá también tengas que pedir al personal de ese Servidor que elimine el contenido que conserva en Discord. Si te encuentras en el Espacio Económico Europeo o en el Reino Unido, también puedes dirigirte a tu autoridad local de protección de datos.

Si crees que un menor nos ha proporcionado datos incumpliendo esta política, contáctanos para que los eliminemos.

8. Seguridad
